# Rules audit: 703–707 Turn-Based Actions, State-Based Actions, Flipping a Coin, Rolling a Die, Copying Objects (rules.txt lines 5488–5751)
Audited at engine-rewrite 917180b on 2026-11-09.

## Summary
- Counts: implemented 21, partial 28, missing 52, N/A 13 (114 rules/subrules total).
- Top gaps (most player-visible first):
  - **CR 704.5j (Legend rule)** — `checkStateBasedActions` (`engine.ts:6695-6701`) silently auto-keeps the newest legendary permanent and puts all earlier copies into the graveyard without prompting the controller, leaving the existing `purpose === 'legend_rule'` prompt handler (`engine.ts:2414-2449`, `runtime/choices.ts:136,177`) unreachable dead code — **S**.
  - **CR 707.10 / 707.10c–f (Copying spells and abilities on the stack)** — `compiler/effects.ts:901-906` compiles `copy target ... spell` to `verb: 'copySpell'`, and `runtime/triggers.ts:427` listens for `SpellCopied`, but `copySpell` is absent from `INTERPRETED_VERBS` (`runtime/effects.ts:165-201`), `executeParsedEffect` has no `copy_target_spell` handler, `SpellCopied` is never emitted in non-test code, and `resolveTopOfStack` (`engine.ts:4171`) skips permanent creation when `item.isCopy` is true — **M**.
  - **CR 707.5 / 707.9 (Clone / entering as a copy & "except ..." copy modifications)** — Only cards matching the legacy regex `cardEntersAsCopyOfAnyCreature` (`oracle.ts:113-115`, `engine.ts:4206-4238`) can enter as copies, and when cast without an explicit target they silently auto-copy the highest-CMC creature on the battlefield (`engine.ts:4213-4224`); `except ...` copy modifications (`707.9a–g`), "becomes a copy" on the battlefield (`707.4`), and `applySelfEtbReplacements` on token copies (`engine.ts:5128-5158`, `707.6`) are missing — **M**.
  - **CR 703.4f / 704.5s (Sagas: precombat main lore counter & final-chapter sacrifice SBA)** — `lore` exists in `CounterType` (`types.ts:60`), but `beginPhase('main1')` (`engine.ts:3248-3278, 5894`) never places a lore counter on Sagas (`703.4f`) and `checkStateBasedActions` never sacrifices finished Sagas (`704.5s`) — **M**.
  - **CR 704.5g / 704.5h / 703.4m (Shield counters & regeneration on lethal/deathtouch SBA and combat damage)** — `checkStateBasedActions` (`engine.ts:6666-6680, 6786`) routes lethal-damage and deathtouch destruction directly to `movePermanentToZone` without checking shield counters (`counters.shield`, CR 122.1c) or regeneration/umbra armor, and `executeCombatDamageStep` (`engine.ts:6190-6348`) likewise ignores `counters.shield` and `damagePreventionShield` on combat damage — **S**.
  - **CR 703.3 / 703.4i / 703.4j (Declare attackers & blockers modeled as priority actions instead of step-start TBAs)** — Entering `declare_attackers` (`engine.ts:5907`) or `declare_blockers` (`engine.ts:5913-5918`) grants priority before attackers/blockers are declared; if the active player clicks `pass_priority` (`engine.ts:2093`) or `end_turn` (`engine.ts:2152`) in `declare_attackers`, Goad and `attacksEachCombat` requirements (`engine.ts:1792-1811`) are bypassed completely — **M**.
  - **CR 703.4k (Combat damage assignment order and division)** — `executeCombatDamageStep` (`engine.ts:6190-6202`) automatically assigns lethal damage in the blocker array order without letting the attacking player choose damage assignment order or over-assign damage to earlier blockers — **M**.
  - **CR 703.4n / 703.4p (Cleanup step ordering for human players)** — When a human player has more than maximum hand size in `runCleanupStep` (`engine.ts:6395`), `beginPhase(state, 'cleanup')` runs `clearEndOfTurnAndDamage(state)` (`engine.ts:3264`) *before* queuing the `discard_to_hand_size` prompt (`engine.ts:6398`), ending "until end of turn" effects and clearing marked damage before the discard happens — **S**.
  - **CR 704.6d (Commander zone-change SBA timing and post-choice triggers)** — For human players, `checkStateBasedActions` (`engine.ts:6896-6919`) queues an async `commander_zone_choice` prompt and finishes the SBA loop + `flushTriggers` (`engine.ts:7001`) before the choice is made; when the player answers Yes (`engine.ts:2472`), `moveObject` is called without `bumpVersion: true`, `flushTriggers(state)`, or `checkStateBasedActions(state)` — **S**.
  - **CR 704.5m / 704.5n / 704.5p (Attachment SBAs: player-enchanting Auras, animated Equipment, Fortifications)** — `checkStateBasedActions` (`engine.ts:6619`) looks up `perm.attachedToId` only via `findPermanentOnBoard`, immediately killing any Aura attached to a player; animated Equipment/Auras that are also Creatures or Battles fail to unattach (`704.5p`); and Fortifications (`704.5n`) are not checked for land type or protection — **S**.
  - **CR 704.3 / 704.8 (Intra-pass SBA simultaneity between attachment removal and creature death)** — Within a single SBA pass, Step 2 (`engine.ts:6631-6653`) sets `perm.attachedToId = null` and calls `noteLayerInputsChanged(state)` *before* Step 3 (`engine.ts:6660`) evaluates creature toughness, causing a creature whose toughness depended on an illegally-attached Aura/Equipment to die in the same pass (with pre-pass LKI toughness from `prePassSnapshot` at `engine.ts:6716-6717`) instead of on the next SBA pass — **S**.
  - **CR 705.1–705.3 & CR 706.1–706.6 (Flipping coins and rolling dice)** — Neither the compiler nor the runtime implements coin flips or die rolls despite seeded PRNG support in `runtime/random.ts:21` — **M**.
  - **CR 707.12 / 707.12a (Copying cards in zones and casting the copies)** — Cards that copy an exiled/graveyard card and let you cast the copy (e.g. Isochron Scepter, Mizzix's Mastery, Garth One-Eye `707.13`, Magar `707.14`) have no compiler or runtime support — **L**.

## Silently skipped / approximated behaviour

- **Legend rule auto-keeps newest permanent without asking (`CR 704.5j`)**: In `checkStateBasedActions` (`engine.ts:6695-6701`), when a player controls two or more legendary permanents with the same name, the engine silently keeps `list[list.length - 1]` (the most recently added to `battlefield`) and puts `list[0 .. list.length - 2]` into the graveyard without prompting the player, even though full prompt handling for `purpose === 'legend_rule'` exists in `engine.ts:2414-2449` and `runtime/choices.ts:136,177,542`.
- **Clone auto-picks highest-CMC creature across the board (`CR 707.5`)**: In `resolveTopOfStack` (`engine.ts:4206-4238`), if a Clone spell resolves without `item.targets[0]` set (per CR 707.5, Clone does not target when cast; the choice is made as it enters), the engine sorts all phased-in creatures across all battlefields by `b.card.cmc` descending and silently copies `allCreatures[0]` (`engine.ts:4213-4224`) without prompting the human controller or allowing the "You may" choice to be declined.
- **Copying a face-down creature reveals and copies its hidden face (`CR 707.2`)**: Both Clone (`engine.ts:4226-4228`) and `copyPermanentAsToken` (`engine.ts:5134-5135`) copy `target.copiableCard ?? target.card` without checking `target.isFaceDown`, turning the copy into the face-down permanent's true printed card instead of a 2/2 colorless nameless creature with no abilities.
- **Combat damage assignment order is auto-decided (`CR 703.4k`)**: In `executeCombatDamageStep` (`engine.ts:6190-6202`), when an attacker is blocked by multiple creatures, the engine assigns lethal damage in `atkEntry.blockerInstanceIds` array order (the order the defending player declared them) without prompting the attacking player to order blockers or choose how much damage (>= lethal) to assign to each blocker.
- **Untap step auto-untaps all eligible permanents (`CR 703.4c`)**: In `executeUntapTurnBasedActions` (`engine.ts:1044-1056`), every tapped permanent without `doesntUntap` or stun counters is automatically untapped with no prompt for "You may choose not to untap ~" abilities.
- **Declare attackers can be skipped via `pass_priority` or `end_turn` (`CR 703.4i`)**: Because declaring attackers is only validated inside `case 'declare_attackers':` (`engine.ts:1699-1811`), a player holding priority at the start of `declare_attackers` can pass priority (`engine.ts:2093`) or end the turn (`engine.ts:2152`) without ever declaring attackers, silently skipping Goad and `attacksEachCombat` requirements.
- **Cleanup step clears damage and EOT buffs before human discard (`CR 703.4n`, `703.4p`)**: In `runCleanupStep` (`engine.ts:6395`), `beginPhase(state, 'cleanup', activePlayer)` is called before pushing the `discard_to_hand_size` prompt (`engine.ts:6398`), which executes `clearEndOfTurnAndDamage(state)` (`engine.ts:3264`) before the human selects which cards to discard.
- **Compiler silently strips `"It can't be regenerated"` (`CR 704.5g`, `704.5h`)**: `compiler/effects.ts:111-112` and `compiler/adapter.ts:159-160` strip `/^(?:it|they) can't be regenerated\.?$/i` as a no-op comment because regeneration is unimplemented, and `checkStateBasedActions` (`engine.ts:6666-6680, 6786`) also skips shield counters (`counters.shield`) when destroying creatures via lethal/deathtouch damage SBAs.
- **Compiler marks `copySpell` cards as supported when runtime cannot execute them (`CR 707.10`)**: `compiler/effects.ts:901-906` compiles `copy target ... spell` into `verb: 'copySpell'` with `supported = true` (so `compileCard` marks the card `fullySupported: true`), but `runtime/effects.ts:211` rejects `copySpell` because it is not in `INTERPRETED_VERBS`, falling back to legacy/manual resolution.

## Legacy-only findings (verify after step F)

- **Clone ("enter the battlefield as a copy of any creature") is legacy-only (`CR 707.5`)**: Detected solely by the regex helper `cardEntersAsCopyOfAnyCreature` in `oracle.ts:113-115` and handled inline in `resolveTopOfStack` (`engine.ts:4206-4238`). There is no IR representation for "enters as a copy" in `ir.ts` or `compiler/statics.ts`.
- **Legacy `copy_target_permanent` & `copy_target_spell` (`CR 707.1`, `707.10`)**: `oracle.ts:1249-1251` parses `create a token that's a copy of it` into `copy_target_permanent` (adapted in `compiler/adapter.ts:1118-1119` and executed in `engine.ts:4512-4517`; this one also has an IR counterpart `copyPermanent` in `compiler/effects.ts:893-899` and `runtime/effects.ts:836-842`). `copy_target_spell` exists in `types.ts:322` and `compiler/adapter.ts:1120-1121`, but has no implementation in either `executeParsedEffect` or `runtime/effects.ts`.
