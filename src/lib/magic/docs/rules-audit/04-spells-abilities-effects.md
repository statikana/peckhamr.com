# Rules audit: 6. Spells, Abilities, and Effects (rules.txt lines 2456–3203)
Audited at engine-rewrite 917180b on 2026-11-09.

## Summary
- Counts: implemented 91, partial 113, missing 100, N/A 15 (total 319 numbered rules and subrules across CR 600–616).
- Top gaps (most player-visible first):
  - **CR 601.2b / 700.2** — Modal spells and abilities have no mode-choice UI, payload field, or IR interpreter support; `validateChosenTargets` (`engine.ts:7626-7643`) merely checks whether *any* modal branch fits the chosen targets, and `runtime/effects.ts:249` rejects any compiled ability with `modal` (`if (parse.modal) return false`) — **M**.
  - **CR 605.3a / 605.4a** — Auto-tapping lands/permanents during spell/ability cost payment (`simulateOrExecuteAutoTap`, `engine.ts:813-819`) directly mutates `perm.tapped = true` and `pool[color] += amount` without emitting `TappedForMana`, so triggered mana abilities (e.g. mana doublers, *Mirari's Wake*, *Crypt Ghast*) never trigger when auto-tapping — **S**.
  - **CR 602.5b / 601.3** — Activation and cast timing/usage restrictions matched in `RESTRICTION_SENTENCES` (`compiler/effects.ts:117-125`, including `"Activate only once each turn"`, `"Activate only during your upkeep"`, `"Cast ~ only during combat"`, `"Cast ~ only before attackers are declared"`) are stripped as no-ops by the compiler and never enforced in `checkAbilityActivationLegality` (`engine.ts:8055`) or `checkSpellCastLegality` (`engine.ts:7901`) — **S**.
  - **CR 610.3 / 610.4 / 607.2a** — Linked exile/phase-out durations (`"Exile target ... until ~ leaves the battlefield"`, *Banisher Priest*, *Oblivion Ring*, *Grasp of Fate*) and linked `"cards exiled with ~"` tracking have no state representation or return-on-leave one-shot mechanism — **M**.
  - **CR 616.1 / 616.1a–f** — When multiple replacement effects apply to the same event (e.g. *Rest in Peace* vs. another graveyard replacement, or multiple draw replacements), the engine does not prompt the affected player or object controller to choose order; `checkGraveyardReplacementZone` (`runtime/replace.ts:724-730`) and `replaceEmptyLibraryDraw` (`runtime/replace.ts:742-750`) take the first match in board order — **M**.
  - **CR 611.2a–c / 613.1b–e** — Continuous effects from resolving spells/abilities are stored only as ad-hoc per-instance counters/arrays (`tempPowerMod`, `tempToughnessMod`, `grantedKeywords`, `switchedPTCount` on `CardInstance`, cleared at cleanup) rather than timestamped continuous-effect objects: layer 2 control-changing effects (*Act of Treason*, *Control Magic*), layer 4/5 type/color changes from resolving spells, layer 7b base-P/T setting from resolving spells, and `"for as long as ..."` durations (CR 611.2b) are missing — **L**.
  - **CR 615.1–615.13** — Damage prevention effects are largely unimplemented: `damagePreventionShield` (`types.ts:383, 784`) and `preventAllCombatDamageThisTurn` (`types.ts:785`) are checked in `runtime/effects.ts:1136, 1199` and `engine.ts:6093`, but nothing in the compiler or runtime ever sets them (only static Protection and Shield counters prevent damage), and combat damage (`engine.ts:6090-6195`) never checks `damagePreventionShield` — **M**.
  - **CR 608.3f** — A copy of a permanent spell (`item.isCopy === true`) does not become a token permanent on resolution; `resolveStackItemNow` (`engine.ts:4171`) guards permanent ETB behind `if (item.kind === 'spell' && !item.isCopy)`, so copied permanent spells simply vanish when they finish resolving — **S**.
  - **CR 613.8** — General layer dependency is not implemented; `runtime/layers.ts:1166-1245` hardcodes a two-pass Layer 4 order (Pass B global type-overwriting *Blood Moon* before Pass C additive land types *Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth*) instead of evaluating CR 613.8a–c dependencies across layers — **L**.
  - **CR 603.2e / 603.2h / 603.8 / 603.12** — Triggered ability subtypes including `"becomes tapped"` / `"gainLife"` / `"drawCard"` (compiled in `compiler/triggers.ts:170, 237, 244` but marked `event_not_implemented` at `compiler/triggers.ts:417` and never emitted), `"Do this only once each turn"` (CR 603.2h), state triggers (CR 603.8), and reflexive `"When you do"` triggers (CR 603.12) are not implemented — **M**.
  - **CR 608.2e–f** — Multi-player effects (`"Each player sacrifices a creature"`, `"Each opponent discards a card"`) iterate in `state.players` seat order (`runtime/effects.ts:686-690`) rather than APNAP order (CR 608.2e–f), and pause/resume (`runtime/effects.ts:1388-1404`) executes each player's sacrifice immediately as they answer rather than collecting all choices in APNAP order and then acting simultaneously — **M**.
  - **CR 601.2d / 608.2d** — Dividing or distributing damage or counters among multiple targets (`"deals 3 damage divided as you choose among one, two, or three targets"`, min 1 per target) is not supported at cast time or resolution — **M**.

## Silently skipped / approximated behaviour

- **Auto-tapping for mana skips `TappedForMana` events (`engine.ts:813-819`)**: `simulateOrExecuteAutoTap` sets `perm.tapped = true` and increments `pool[chosenColor]` directly without emitting `TappedForMana`, silently skipping any triggered mana abilities (*Mirari's Wake*, *Nissa, Who Shakes the World*) when a player casts a spell or activates an ability without manually tapping each land first.
- **Auto-tapping ignores non-`{T}` mana abilities (`engine.ts:674-687`)**: `tapOnlyManaAbilities` filters out mana abilities with any cost other than `{T}` (e.g. Treasure tokens `{T}, Sacrifice ~`, painlands that pay life, filter lands, *Phyrexian Tower*), so players must manually activate them or use `tap_mana`.
- **`tap_mana` does not check priority and auto-picks the first mana ability (`engine.ts:1234-1255`)**: `tap_mana` never checks `priorityPlayer.playerId === actor.playerId` (allowing mana activation during an opponent's priority) and selects `(preferred && manaAbs.find((a) => a.producesMana.includes(preferred))) || manaAbs[0]` without allowing the player to choose which mana ability of a permanent to activate.
- **Flexible (hybrid / Phyrexian) mana payment is auto-resolved without player choice (`oracle.ts:209-255`, `engine.ts:775-788`)**: Players never announce whether they are paying 2 life or colored mana for Phyrexian symbols (`{W/P}`) or which half of a hybrid symbol (`{W/U}`, `{2/W}`) they are paying; `simulateOrExecuteAutoTap` pays colored mana if available from pool/untapped lands and automatically pays 2 life per remaining Phyrexian symbol if not.
- **Activation and cast restrictions stripped as no-ops (`compiler/effects.ts:117-125`)**: The compiler explicitly matches `"Activate only once each turn."`, `"Activate only during your upkeep."`, `"Cast ~ only during combat."`, `"Cast ~ only during your turn."`, and `"Cast ~ only before attackers are declared."` in `RESTRICTION_SENTENCES` so cards with those lines compile as supported, but `checkAbilityActivationLegality` (`engine.ts:8055`) and `checkSpellCastLegality` (`engine.ts:7901`) never enforce `"once each turn"`, `"during your upkeep"`, `"during combat"`, or `"before attackers are declared"`.
- ***Grand Abolisher*'s ability-activation lock is parsed into a cast-only rule (`compiler/statics.ts:382-385`, `runtime/replace.ts:403-413`)**: `"During your turn, your opponents can't cast spells or activate abilities of artifacts, creatures, or enchantments"` compiles to `opponentsCantCastDuringYourTurn`, which is checked only in `checkGlobalCastPermissionsAndRestrictions` (`engine.ts:7941`), leaving opponents free to activate abilities of artifacts, creatures, and enchantments during your turn.
- **Clone auto-picks highest-CMC creature if untargeted (`engine.ts:4206-4223`)**: If a `cardEntersAsCopyOfAnyCreature` spell resolves without `item.targets[0]`, the engine does not prompt a human controller; it sorts all creatures on the battlefield by `b.card.cmc` descending and copies `allCreatures[0]`.
- **Multi-player effects iterate in seat order instead of APNAP order and execute sacrifices non-simultaneously (`runtime/effects.ts:686-690, 1388-1404`)**: `each_player` and `each_opponent` iterate `state.players` in seat index order rather than APNAP order (CR 608.2e–f), and when multiple humans must choose a permanent to sacrifice, each player's chosen permanent is sacrificed before the next player is prompted.
- **Multiple replacement effects pick first in board order (`runtime/replace.ts:724-730, 742-750`)**: `checkGraveyardReplacementZone` and `replaceEmptyLibraryDraw` return the first matching replacement effect in `applicableReplacements` without asking the affected player/controller (CR 616.1).
- **Multiple trigger-doubling effects multiply instead of adding (`runtime/replace.ts:952`)**: `triggerMultiplier` computes `factor *= def.factor` (`2 * 2 = 4`) instead of adding additional triggers (`1 + 1 + 1 = 3`, CR 603.2d).
- **Ward auto-pays (with auto-tap) or counters without prompting the spell's controller (`engine.ts:4130-4165`)**: When a `ward_trigger` resolves, it calls `canPayOrAutoTap` and immediately pays the ward cost if payable (even for a human player who might want to decline payment and let their spell be countered!).
- **Runaway trigger safety caps silently drop triggers past 300 stack items or 200 triggers/turn (`engine.ts:3510-3516, 3597-3620`)**: Intentional safety guard against infinite loops, logged to the game log.

## Legacy-only findings (verify after step F)

- **Activated ability cost modifiers (`evaluateActionNode`, `ruleTree.ts:849-980`, called at `engine.ts:8085`)**: Cost increases/reductions on activated abilities (e.g. Equip cost reductions) are evaluated through `ruleTree.ts` `collectActiveBoardHooks` (`hook.cost_modifier`) in `checkAbilityActivationLegality` and `activate_ability`, whereas `runtime/costs.ts` `effectiveSpellManaCost` (`runtime/costs.ts:158-236`) only handles *spell* cost modifiers (`def.affects.k === 'self'` or `'spell'`, skipping `def.affects.k === 'ability'`). When `ruleTree.ts` is deleted in step F, `def.affects.k === 'ability'` in `compiler/statics.ts:436-441` must be wired into `runtime/costs.ts`!
- **Spell targeting and modal branch validation (`parseSpellOrAbilityEffects`, `oracle.ts`, `engine.ts:1465-1479, 7626-7643`)**: `cast_spell` and `activate_ability` still use legacy `parseSpellOrAbilityEffects(inst.card).targetInfo` from `oracle.ts` for cast-time target validation (`validateChosenTargets`), rather than compiled IR `TargetSpec[]` (which is currently only used by triggered abilities in `putTriggerOnStack`, `engine.ts:3528-3532`).
- **Ward trigger resolution (`ward_trigger` in `executeParsedEffect`, `engine.ts:4130-4165`)**: Ward triggers still resolve via the legacy `executeParsedEffect` `case 'ward_trigger'` path (`engine.ts:3766, 4056`).
- **Delayed end-step trigger payloads (`DelayedTrigger.effects: ParsedEffect[]`, `types.ts:518`, `runtime/effects.ts:1598, 1612`, `engine.ts:3223`)**: Even when `runtime/effects.ts` (`delayed`, lines 1577–1621) creates a `DelayedTrigger`, it populates legacy `ParsedEffect[]` (`exile_target`, `lose_the_game_self`) which resolve via `executeParsedEffect` when the delayed trigger resolves.
- **Clone ETB copy detection (`cardEntersAsCopyOfAnyCreature` in `oracle.ts:1922`, called at `engine.ts:4206`)**: Clone-style ETB replacement on resolving creature spells is detected by legacy `cardEntersAsCopyOfAnyCreature` in `oracle.ts` rather than compiled `ReplacementDef` in `ir.ts`.
- **Self-exiling spells (`spellExilesSelfOnResolution` in `oracle.ts:1915`, called at `engine.ts:4286`)**: Checked alongside `result.exileSelf` from the IR interpreter when moving a resolved instant/sorcery from the stack.
- **Extra land play statics (`getCardExtraLandPlays` in `oracle.ts`, called in `checkLandPlayLegality` at `engine.ts:7890`)**: Checked via `oracle.ts` rather than a compiled `StaticDef` in `runtime/replace.ts`.
